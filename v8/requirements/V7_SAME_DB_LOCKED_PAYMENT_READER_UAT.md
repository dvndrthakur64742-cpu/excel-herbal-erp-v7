# Excel Herbal ERP V8 v0.18 — Same-DB Locked Sale/Payment Bridge (Development)

## Why this matters

The v0.17 same-connection canonical writer had a **trusted locked-reader callback port**, but it had no concrete adapter to connect issued V8 UAT quote/tender evidence to the existing V7 StockService + AccountingService. A browser-provided `posting_allowed=true`, screenshot, UTR or precomputed quote never constitutes a payment confirmation.

`V7UatLockedCheckoutReader::__invoke($actor,$trustedContext,$wpdb)` now fills that **development-only** port. It is designed to be invoked *inside* `V7CanonicalPostingSinkUat::post`'s `Transaction::run` using **the exact same WordPress DB connection**. It validates the WP user actor/company/branch and refuses non-development/non-`v8_test` environments through the existing writer guard. It does not register a REST route or enable posting.

## Row-lock / source chain

1. Lock server-issued `v8_b2b_sale_intents_uat`, `v8_b2b_issued_sale_context_uat`, every immutable `v8_b2b_sale_intent_lines_uat` row; recalculate line arithmetic and SHA-256 digest over the original quote including company, branch, canonical party, retailer, channel, employee custody, business timestamp, payment method, total and ordered lines.
2. Lock `v8_b2b_sale_tender_reservations_uat`, `v8_b2b_payment_intent_to_sale_uat`, `v8_b2b_payment_intents_uat`, `v8_b2b_tender_intake_evidence_uat`, `v8_b2b_verified_tenders`; require one-to-one sale/payment/tender binding, exact INR paise, channel/custodian/source/reference, positive attesting actor and unconsumed confirmed tender.
3. Lock the **separate independently verified** `eh7_b2b_verified_tenders` bridge row. Match quote digest, payment intent and tender IDs, trusted reference, source, verified actor, exact amount, company/branch, currency and not-yet-consumed state. **This bridge is not generated by the reader or a checkout.** Actual verified PSP/cashier ingestion capable of populating it is still missing. Its presence alone is not a proof of external PSP verification unless that separate authenticated ingestor is implemented and tested.
4. Re-read **actual V7** branch, employee, products, batch, effective price revisions, stock custody, accounting period, branch closing and Chart of Accounts under `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the same connection using `V7UatRowLockReader`. Validate price distribution/MRP, whole PCS, expiry, average-cost COGS and exact priced product/price-revision snapshot through `CanonicalV7Readiness`.
5. Return exactly the `assessment`, `quote` and `tender` structures required by `V7CanonicalPostingSinkUat`, with an internal-only `posting_allowed=true` after all gates. The sink still checks V7 retailer/customer identity and outstanding legacy receivables; posts one canonical sale source, canonical V7 stock and balanced GL; and atomically consumes one bridge tender inside its existing transaction.

## Source schema

`009_v7_canonical_retailer_posting_DRAFT.sql` previously lacked `currency`, `status`, `payment_intent_id`, `tender_id`, `verified_by`, `channel` and `employee_id` on the canonical tender bridge, despite the v0.17 consumer relying on some of these. These draft-only fields are now consistent with the reader and enforced by indexes/constraints. **Never apply or replace this draft schema on a working database.** It assumes disposable `v8_test` with `wp_` prefix and pre-created V7+V8 isolated fixtures; it is not an installable migration.
